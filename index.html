About

I've been a CRM engineer my whole career, starting with Dynamics CRM 2011 at xrm.com in 2012. I then spent more than eight years at Collaborate Solutions building CRM solutions for clients in banking, agriculture and the public sector, Farm Credit Bank of Texas among them. In 2021 I joined Farm Credit Bank of Texas directly, where I'm part of the team that builds and looks after its Dynamics 365 platform.

Over the years my work has grown from forms and plugins to the bigger picture: integrations, APIs, cloud services, and lately AI features built with RAG and MCP. Outside of work I build free tools for the Dynamics 365 community and volunteer with nonprofits.
